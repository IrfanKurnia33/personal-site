Here we are again at the end of a somewhat wild year for me. Here is my 2025, in 25 bullet points:

![My and wife for our anniversary](/blog-images/2025.webp)

* Started the year with my extended family, I had good drinks with my cousins and heart crazy stories about growing up in West Jakarta.
* Entered 2025 as an unemployed. To be honest it felt scary.
* Tweaked my portfolio aggressively. I can't remember how many times I destroyed my portfolio and started again from scratch.
* Achieved one of my major lifetime dreams to watch Green Day live in Jakarta with my band and childhood best friend.
* Tore my ankle in a moshpit when Green Day played St. Jimmy. The medical tent was really fast, respect to them.
* Got a freelance project from an old colleague in a software agency.
* In a circle of doubt about career. Really didn't know at all about where my design career would go. Been thinking about other options.
* Celebrated my wife's employment again after a year. I really adore and admire her spirit in building our little family.
* Started [Walkfuzz](https://www.instagram.com/walkfuzz), a media platform that talks about stocks. Didn't know much about stocks but it's a good way to save money.
* In April, I finally got a full-time job again with the recommendation of a friend.
* Experienced working in the media industry for the first time. After two years working remotely, it felt strange to have friends from the office again.
* Celebrated my birthday with an awesome SUPR burger my wife bought as the birthday cake. She knows me too well lol.
* Got surprised by my band at a fast food chain, celebrated my birthday like a kid.
* Celebrated my wife's birthday in a nice hotel in Central Jakarta. Had an Italian dinner which is kinda nice.
* Got close friends to surprise my wife as well. It was amazing with many gifts.
* Finally released a mini album with my band after years of working on it.
* Felt kinda bummed because my band were supposed to have a solo showcase, inviting close friends and family.
* Been sketching and writing stuff for my next musical project. But so far came up with none. Perhaps in 2026.
* Celebrated our wedding anniversary by watching Rossa. Phenomenal singer, the topic kinda didn't match with the vibe lol.
* My wife is also thinking of starting her own music project. Had fun exploring options but then again... maybe in 2026.
* Got a nice flight discount so my wife and I went to Bali. Her experience stepping outside the Java island.
* Experienced a beach club for the first time. Bali is truly different honestly.
* Minted our own new wedding rings at a Bali workshop. It now has more sentimental value than ever.
* I guess 2025 is the year I got less sleep than before (at least on my way to become 30).
* Celebrated 2026 with my closest friends. This time we're having a nice staycation in West Jakarta.

Here's to a wonderful 2026. Happy new year y'all! 🎉
